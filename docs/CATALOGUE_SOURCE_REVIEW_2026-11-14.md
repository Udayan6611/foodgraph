**Review type:** external evidence / read-only

## Scope and method

A fresh read-only Supabase query returned **55 location records with at least one claim linked to a non-empty source URL**, spanning **54 distinct URLs and 81 claim-to-source links**. This is a source-linked subset, not the entire 162-row catalogue.

The review compared the recorded value and provider label with the linked page when retrievable, then with a current official business page or other provider listing where available. Google Maps URLs in the catalogue are search pointers, not canonical place IDs. Search-result snippets and third-party aggregators are treated as leads, not as primary evidence. Rating values are always provider-specific; Google, District, Zomato, Swiggy and Tripadvisor numbers are not interchangeable.

The ratings and review counts below are observations from pages retrieved during this review on 14 November 2026. Counts naturally change over time. Some source pages were not retrievable or did not expose all fields; those cases remain unresolved.

## Price and cuisine handling

- The legacy price tiers 1–4 are explicitly marked in the database as prototype tiers, not INR prices. **None has been converted into a real price.**
- Current menu or “for two” values found on District, Zomato, Swiggy or official sites are provider/menu observations. They are not a substitute for a normalized numeric price range unless the price basis is captured.
- Cuisine associations are not independently verified merely because they appear in the imported row. Keep them as candidate tags until a direct restaurant menu or other suitable source supports them.
