FOODGRAPH · YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy Policy.

This page explains what information the current FoodGraph website handles, why it is used, where it is stored and how you can contact us about it.

Last updated: 9 November 2026Applies to: foodgraph.dpdns.org and its FoodGraph pages

1. Who operates FoodGraph

FoodGraph is a Pune-focused restaurant discovery product currently operated by its founder. It is an early-stage product, not a restaurant, booking service or food-delivery business.

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or questions about this policy, contact founder@foodgraph.dpdns.org.

2. Information we handle

The current website works without an account. Depending on how you use it, FoodGraph may handle the following information:

A. Choices and feedback in the discovery experience

  • The Pune area you select.
  • Cuisines and food preferences you select.
  • Your selected budget range and dining vibe.
  • Your responses to recommendations, such as “Useful” or “Not for me”, and the restaurant to which that feedback relates.

These preferences and feedback signals are stored in your browser so that the discovery experience can remember your choices and adjust later recommendations.

B. Product events and referral information

When you use the discovery experience, the website can send event information to our Google Apps Script endpoint. Depending on the action, this may include:

  • Event type, such as a page view, taste submission, result view, restaurant click or feedback action.
  • A browser-generated session identifier.
  • The page or route you visited.
  • Your selected area, cuisines, budget and vibes.
  • Restaurant names and recommendation scores shown in a result list, or the restaurant you clicked or rated.
  • Referral details, including referrer ID, source and campaign, where a referral link was used.

We use this information to operate and evaluate the product, understand how people find FoodGraph and improve the recommendation experience. A session identifier is not an account or password, but it can help group activity from the same browser session.

C. Early-access signup

If you use the early-access form, we collect the name and email address you enter, an optional area, the timestamp of the submission, and the session and referral fields carried by the form (where available). The form also records whether the optional product-updates checkbox was selected.

The checkbox is optional. Leaving it unchecked does not prevent a signup; the form records the choice as “no”. The current form code records this preference but does not itself run an automated newsletter or mailing service. We will not treat an unchecked box as permission for promotional email updates.

The signup records are stored in a separate “Signups” sheet from product events. The form includes session and referral details, so some signup activity may still be understood in its referral or session context.

D. Information we do not ask for in the current MVP

FoodGraph currently does not ask you to create an account or provide a password, phone number, payment information, contacts, or precise device location. Area selection is a choice you make in the interface; it is not GPS tracking.

3. Browser storage

FoodGraph uses your browser’s local storage (LocalStorage), rather than requiring an account, to remember items such as your session ID, referral attribution, selected discovery preferences and recommendation feedback. These values can remain in the browser until you clear the site’s stored data. The current MVP does not set an automatic expiry period for these values.

You can remove this browser-stored information by clearing the site data or LocalStorage for FoodGraph in your browser settings. Doing so may reset saved preferences and feedback. Clearing browser data does not delete event or signup rows already sent to our Google Sheet; use the contact details below to request deletion of those records.

4. Why we use the information

  • To provide restaurant discovery and rank places against the choices you make.
  • To remember feedback and preferences in your browser.
  • To understand whether the discovery flow and recommendations are useful and to improve the product.
  • To measure broad product usage and referral sources.
  • To manage early-access registrations and, only where you selected “yes”, any occasional product updates we may send.
  • To detect basic automated or abusive form submissions and protect the service.

We do not sell personal information. The current MVP does not use third-party advertising or cross-site ad-targeting tools.

5. Where information is stored and services we use

  • Your browser: discovery preferences, feedback, session and referral values are stored in LocalStorage on the device and browser you use.
  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets: product events and early-access form submissions are sent to our Google Apps Script web app and written to the project’s Google Sheets spreadsheet. The Events and Signups records are kept in separate sheets.
  • GitHub Pages: the public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information as part of delivering the website.
  • Google Maps: choosing “Open in Maps” takes you to Google Maps using a restaurant search. Google then handles that visit under its own terms and privacy practices.

These providers operate their own services and policies. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Their infrastructure and processing arrangements may involve locations outside India.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell signup details or product-event information. Information is handled through the service providers described above to operate the site and its backend. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or to protect the service and people using it.

The Google Sheets spreadsheet is intended to remain private. Access to it should be restricted to the founder and any person who genuinely needs access to operate FoodGraph.

7. How long we keep information

Browser-stored values remain until you clear them or remove the relevant site data. Product events and signup records are kept in the project’s Google Sheets. The current MVP does not yet apply an automatic deletion schedule to those spreadsheet records; they may remain until manually deleted or a deletion request is processed.

We intend to keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described here, and to remove or de-identify it when it is no longer needed, subject to legitimate operational or legal requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use the discovery or signup features. You can clear browser-stored preferences using your browser settings. You can also contact us to ask what signup or event information we hold, request a correction, ask for deletion, or change or withdraw your product-updates preference.

Email founder@foodgraph.dpdns.org from the address used for signup, where possible. Please describe the request and include enough information for us to locate the relevant record. We may ask a reasonable follow-up question to verify the request. Please do not email passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information unless we specifically explain a lawful need for it.

We will review and respond to requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law and the need to retain limited records where required.

9. Security

We use the controls available in the services that host the website and store its data, and aim to limit access to project records. No website, browser or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through FoodGraph.

10. Children

FoodGraph is intended for adults aged 18 and above. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to FoodGraph, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the product, the information it handles or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
