01 / THE PROBLEM

Average taste is a terrible recommendation engine.

Search any restaurant and you get the same answer as everyone else: stars, reviews, photos and a popularity score.

But your perfect Saturday dinner might be someone else's skip. FoodGraph starts with a different question: what kind of place fits the way you eat?
