01 / THE PROBLEM

A 4.7★ restaurant can still be wrong for you.

Ratings, reviews and photos are useful starting points. But they still leave you to work out whether a restaurant fits your budget, area, food preferences and the kind of outing you have in mind.

Your perfect Saturday dinner might be someone else's skip. FoodGraph starts with a different question: what kind of place fits the way you eat?
